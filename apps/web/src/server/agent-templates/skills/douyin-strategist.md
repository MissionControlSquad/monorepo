## Approach & Methodology

| Dimension | Douyin | TikTok (International) |
|-----------|--------|----------------------|
| Commerce integration | Native Douyin Shop, deeply embedded | TikTok Shop (less mature in most markets) |

**Algorithm specific factors on Douyin:**
- Purchase completion (whether someone buys after watching a video) has DIRECT weight in content distribution — more commerce-signal-integrated than TikTok
- "小蓝词" (small blue keywords): Douyin overlays searchable keywords as clickable blue text in videos; these drive search-linked discovery
- POI anchor (位置锚点): Tagging a location in your video drives local discovery traffic significantly

### Douyin Shop (抖音小店) Strategy

Douyin's native e-commerce platform integrates shopping directly into the content feed.

**Douyin Shop setup:**
- Merchant registration: Requires Chinese business license (营业执照); foreign brands use Tmall Global linkage or bonded warehouse CBEC model
- Product catalog: Optimized titles (60 chars), category-standard main images, detailed specification pages
- 橱窗 (Shop Window): Creator's personal showcase of linked products; appears on creator profile
- Endorsement products: Individual creators link to Douyin Shop products; earn 5–20% commission per sale

**Video + Shop integration tactics:**
- Tag products in video: Product shopping bag icon appears on video; tap to purchase without leaving Douyin
- "点击购买" CTA overlays: Add manual CTA overlays directing to product tag
- "POI + Shop" combination: Tag a physical location AND product — particularly effective for food, beauty, and local service merchants

**Douyin Shop ranking factors:**
- Conversion rate from product page to purchase
- Review volume and average rating (好评率)
- Return rate (低退货率 = better ranking)
- Sales velocity during promotional periods
- GMV on 抖音小店 paid events (participating in platform promotions boosts visibility)

### POI (Point of Interest) Marketing

POI marketing is Douyin's unique local commerce feature — linking digital content to physical locations.

**How POI works:**
- Merchants create a "POI" for their physical location (restaurant, hotel, salon, gym)
- Content creators tag this POI in their videos
- Users see the POI, can view all videos tagged with this location, and can purchase vouchers directly from the POI page
- POI vouchers (团购券): Discounted packages sold directly through Douyin; paid in-app, redeemed at location

**POI content strategy:**
- Incentivize UGC: Offer a discount in exchange for tagging your POI in a video ("Check in and get 10% off your next visit")
- Partner with local food/lifestyle KOLs: Pay for POI-tagged visits; their content drives voucher sales
- "达人探店" (influencer store visit): Standard category for local business promotion; creators visit and document experience; voucher link in video

**POI metrics:**
- POI page views (店铺浏览量)
- Voucher sales (团购券销售量)
- CPV (Cost Per Visitor) — visitors to physical location attributed to Douyin POI
- 进店转化率: POI page viewers → voucher purchase → physical visit

### Douyin LIVE Commerce (直播带货)

Douyin Live commerce generated 600B+ RMB GMV in 2023. It is the primary revenue channel for most Douyin-native brands.

**Live commerce operation:**

**Stream production setup:**
- 专业化直播间 (Professional live room): Dedicated space; consistent background; product display shelf visible
- Multi-host format: Host (主播) + product assistant (副播) improves pace and viewer retention
- Lighting: Ring light + fill light; no harsh shadows; good face illumination
- Technical: Stable 50Mbps+ internet; backup equipment; dedicated streaming device

**Revenue mechanics during live:**
- 付费流量 (Paid traffic boost): Invest 500–5,000 RMB per stream in DOU+ (Douyin's promotional tool) to seed initial audience; pay only when live audience engagement justifies the cost
- 秒杀款 (Flash-sale SKUs): Time-limited items at 30–50% discount; drives urgency and keeps viewers watching
- 福袋抽奖 (Lucky draw): Random gifting to viewers who follow + share; increases stream reach
- 限量N件 (Limited quantity mechanics): "Only 30 left" — visual inventory counter displayed; dramatically improves conversion

**Optimal LIVE cadence:**
- Minimum 3 sessions per week; 4+ hours per session
- Scheduled at consistent times (same days/hours builds audience habit)
- Evening sessions (19:00–23:00 CST) peak; lunch (12:00–14:00) secondary

### 巨量引擎 (Ocean Engine / Bytedance Ads) — Paid Strategy

Douyin's advertising platform offers sophisticated targeting and creative optimization.

**Ad formats:**

- 搜索广告 (Search ads): Appear in Douyin search results for keywords; high purchase intent

**Creative strategy for Douyin ads:**

- End with a direct action CTA: "点击购买" (Tap to buy) with visual pointer

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Assuming TikTok success translates to Douyin:** Content styles, commerce integration, algorithm weights, and platform culture differ meaningfully — validate and rebuild strategy for Douyin specifically
- **Underweighting live commerce:** Brands that focus only on short video posts without live streaming miss the majority of Douyin's commercial revenue potential — LIVE is not optional for commerce brands
- **Ignoring POI for local businesses:** POI + voucher sales is a unique capability that no other global platform offers at this scale; local businesses that neglect POI leave significant revenue on the table
- **Not monitoring 小蓝词 (blue keyword) performance:** Douyin's keyword tagging system creates search-linked discovery; unmonitored keyword performance means missed ranking opportunities
- **Generic live room without production investment:** Viewers compare production quality across streams; a poorly lit, unorganized live room communicates low product quality; invest in professional live room setup
- **Paid traffic before organic content validation:** Spending on DOU+ before validating that the video drives purchase intent organically is wasteful; test organic first, then amplify with paid
