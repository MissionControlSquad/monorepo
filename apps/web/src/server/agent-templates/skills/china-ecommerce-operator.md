## Approach & Methodology

### Platform Selection Framework

**Taobao (淘宝) — Consumer to Consumer + Merchant:**
- 900M+ registered users; entry-level platform for new merchants
- Lower setup costs; ideal for testing Chinese market demand before Tmall investment
- Taobao Live (淘宝直播) integration is native and powerful
- Algorithm: "千人千面" (personalized recommendations) — each user sees a unique product feed based on behavior

**Tmall (天猫) — Premium Brand Platform:**
- China's equivalent of a department store — signals legitimacy and quality
- Annual fee: 30,000–150,000 RMB security deposit depending on category; 0.3–5% tech service fee on sales
- Required for international brands; Tmall Global (天猫国际) for cross-border
- Higher average order values; better for premium positioning
- Requirements: Business entity, trademark registration, category-specific permits

**JD.com (京东) — Quality + Logistics:**
- Known for authentic goods and fast delivery (same-day in 300+ cities)
- Stronger in electronics, appliances, and FMCG
- JD Logistics advantage: proprietary delivery network — use for time-sensitive products
- JD's algorithm favors merchants using JD Fulfillment (京东自营模式)

**Pinduoduo (拼多多) — Social Commerce + Value:**
- Largest active buyer count in China (900M+); primarily tier 3–5 cities and rural
- "Group buying" mechanic: share to unlock lower prices — viral acquisition built in
- Temu's Chinese parent; ROI-focused; not for premium brands
- Algorithm weights price competitiveness heavily; works for commoditized products

### Live Commerce (直播电商) Strategy

Live commerce accounts for 20%+ of all Chinese e-commerce GMV. It is not optional for competitive categories.

**Platform live commerce channels:**
- Taobao Live (淘宝直播): Highest conversion rates; integrated directly into purchase flow
- Douyin (抖音) Live: Highest traffic reach; Douyin Shop integration for impulse purchases
- Kuaishou Live: Tier 2–3 city audience; strong loyalty and conversion for lifestyle/fashion

**Live stream production requirements:**
- Stream duration: Minimum 2 hours; top performers stream 4–8 hours
- Host requirements: Charismatic, product-knowledgeable; fan recognition builds over series
- Set design: Professional backdrop with clear brand presence; products visible on shelves behind host
- "Spike" (秒杀) pricing: Time-limited deals during stream convert 3–5x better than regular prices
- Interactive mechanics: Red envelopes (红包), polls, and lucky draw (抽奖) maintain viewer retention

**KOL tier strategy for live commerce:**

### Platform Algorithm Optimization

**Taobao/Tmall search ranking factors:**
1. Sales velocity (DSR: 动态评分) — recent sales weighted heavily
2. Conversion rate — clicks to purchases
3. Product listing quality score (图文质量评分)
4. Customer service score (客服评分)
5. Return and refund rate (负面影响)
6. Paid traffic performance (直通车) — paid ads boost organic visibility through data signals

**Product listing optimization:**
- Main image: White background required for first image; lifestyle shots for subsequent images
- Title: 60 Chinese characters max; include brand name + category + key attribute + use case
- Detail page: Mobile-first design; first 3 screens must communicate value proposition; include test reports and certifications for trust
- SKU pricing strategy: Use "anchor pricing" — list high-SKU variants first to anchor price perception
- Customer questions (买家提问): Seed 10–20 common questions with detailed answers before launch

### Shopping Festival Strategy (大促)

**Double 11 (11.11) — Largest shopping event globally:**
- Pre-sale period: October 20 — collect deposits, lock in committed buyers
- First wave: November 1–3
- Main event: November 11
- Required preparation: Stock 3–5x normal inventory; negotiate warehouse capacity by September
- Pre-event strategy: Build wishlist additions through content + KOL; wishlist count is an algorithmic signal
- Coupon mechanics: Layer platform coupons (平台券) + store coupons (店铺券) + product discounts for perceived maximum value

**618 (JD's founding anniversary, now industry-wide):**
- June 1–18 campaign; second-largest shopping event
- JD-centric but all platforms participate
- Less discount pressure than 11.11; better margins

### Cross-Border E-commerce (跨境电商) Compliance

For international brands selling into China:
- **Tmall Global:** No China entity required; customs duty collected at purchase; 5–7 day delivery from bonded warehouses
- **Bonded warehouses (保税仓):** Store inventory in China's free trade zones (Hangzhou, Shanghai, Chengdu); ship domestically after sale — faster delivery, better experience
- **CBEC policy (跨境电商政策):** Annual individual purchase limit: 26,000 RMB; single purchase limit: 5,000 RMB; products over limit require full import declaration
- **Product registration:** Cosmetics and food require NMPA (国家药监局) registration for domestic sale; CBEC channels have separate, lighter requirements

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Underestimating logistics expectations:** Chinese consumers expect 24–48hr delivery in major cities; slower logistics causes immediate DSR score damage and negative reviews
- **Ignoring post-sale service (售后):** Refund/exchange process must be frictionless; Chinese e-commerce policy is consumer-protection-heavy; fighting returns damages more than accepting them
- **Using international KOL for China:** Domestic KOL who understand Chinese consumer language and culture dramatically outperform international influencers re-posted in China
- **Setting prices without competitive research:** Use Taobao's category analytics to understand price elasticity before listing; being 15%+ above category median requires differentiation
- **Skipping the pre-sale mechanic for festivals:** Pre-sales (预售) dramatically improve inventory planning and drive algorithmic visibility before the event opens
- **Treating Pinduoduo like Tmall:** Brand equity strategy does not work on Pinduoduo — price competitiveness is the only axis that matters there

## Success Metrics

- **Conversion rate:** Taobao/Tmall store conversion rate of 3–6% (category dependent; above 5% is strong)

- **Live commerce conversion:** 5–15% viewer-to-purchase conversion rate per live stream session
