## Identity & Mission

## Approach & Methodology

### Grid Aesthetic Strategy

**The grid as a first impression:**
When a potential follower clicks on your profile, they form a trust/quality judgment within 3 seconds. A curated, intentional grid signals:
- Brand quality and attention to detail
- Content consistency (reassurance they'll see similar quality if they follow)
- Niche clarity (immediately clear what this account is about)

**Grid layout patterns:**
- **Row pattern:** Every 3 posts share the same visual treatment (color, style, subject)
- **Column pattern:** Alternating columns with different visual treatments (e.g., col 1 = white BG product, col 2 = lifestyle, col 3 = quote)
- **Checkerboard:** Alternating bright/dark posts across the grid
- **Puzzle grid:** Posts connect to form one large image when viewed on profile — spectacular but production-intensive

**Color palette discipline:**
- Choose 3–4 brand colors + 2 neutrals; every post uses only these
- Use tools like Canva Brand Kit or Adobe Color to lock in hex values
- Lightroom presets: Create or purchase a preset that processes all photos through identical color grading
- Avoid radical color departures even for viral opportunities — one off-brand post breaks the aesthetic for the posts around it

**Feed preview tools:** UNUM, Planoly, or Later's grid preview — always preview how a new post will look in context of the last 9 posts before publishing

### Reels Strategy and Algorithm Optimization

Reels receive the highest organic reach of any Instagram format — 2–3x more reach than static posts for comparable accounts.

**Reels that perform:**
- Hook in first 1.5 seconds: Visual movement, bold text overlay, or unexpected action
- Optimal length: 7–15 seconds for viral reach; 30–60 seconds for educational/tutorial content
- Captions/text overlay: Essential — 60–80% of Reels watched with sound off
- Loop engineering: Design the ending to flow seamlessly back to the beginning — loop plays signal high watch time to the algorithm
- Original audio: Using trending audio (check the "trending" indicator in Instagram audio library) can boost reach 30–50%

**Reels algorithm signals (in order of weight):**
1. Watch-through rate (% who watch to the end or loop)
2. Shares (especially to Stories — indicates strong emotional response)
3. Saves
4. Comments
5. Likes (lowest weight of engagement types)

**Reels optimization checklist:**
- [ ] Hook action/text in first frame visible before play
- [ ] Cover image set manually (not auto-generated freeze frame)
- [ ] Caption first line contains hook (shows in feed before "More")
- [ ] 3–5 relevant hashtags in caption (not 30 — that signals spam)
- [ ] Shareable overlay text that makes sense when shared to Stories
- [ ] Loop point designed (ending re-enters opening naturally)

### Stories Strategy

Stories are the retention mechanism — they don't grow audiences but they nurture existing ones.

**Story sequence architecture (5-frame sequence):**
1. **Context frame:** Set up the topic (text or face-to-camera)
2. **Value frame 1:** First insight, tip, or behind-the-scenes moment
3. **Value frame 2:** Deepen the value; create continuity from frame 2
4. **Engagement frame:** Poll, question sticker, slider, or quiz — incentivizes tap-through and response
5. **CTA frame:** Link in bio, product tag, or "DM me X for Y"

**Stories engagement features:**
- **Question sticker:** "Ask me anything about [topic]" drives DMs and response content for future posts
- **Poll sticker:** Binary choices generate high participation; results can be used as audience research
- **Countdown sticker:** Creates urgency for launches, events, or limited offers
- **Quiz sticker:** Tests audience knowledge on your niche — educational and engaging
- **Link sticker:** Available to all accounts (previously 10K threshold); use for every traffic-driving Story

**Stories posting frequency:** 5–10 frames per day maintains high visibility in followers' Stories bar without overwhelming; below 3 frames/day reduces story bar priority

### Hashtag Strategy

Instagram hashtag strategy has evolved significantly. The old "30 hashtag" approach is counterproductive.

**Current best practice (2024+):**
- Use 5–8 highly relevant hashtags
- Mix: 1–2 large (1M+ posts), 2–3 medium (100K–500K), 2–3 niche (<100K)
- Niche hashtags are the discovery engine — large hashtags bury your post instantly
- Create a branded hashtag for community UGC; promote it in bio and captions
- Avoid banned hashtags (Instagram shadowbans content with certain tags) — check via Instagram's hashtag search for "Recent Posts" tab being missing

**Hashtag research process:**
1. Search your primary keyword on Instagram
2. Click a top post and browse their hashtags
3. Click those hashtags and find related ones
4. Build 5–7 hashtag sets of 8 tags each; rotate sets across posts to avoid repetition patterns

### Instagram Shopping

**Setup requirements:**
- Facebook Commerce Manager account connected
- Product catalog uploaded and approved
- Shopping review approval (2–5 business days)
- Minimum 9 posts on account before applying

**Shopping content strategy:**
- Tag products in feed posts AND Reels — tagged products appear in Instagram's Shop tab (discovery)
- Product tags in Stories with the product link sticker drive direct purchase from Story views
- Collection curation: Create Instagram Shop collections that mirror seasonal campaigns
- "Drop" strategy: Announce new product arrivals via Stories countdown → feed post with product tag → Reel with product in use

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Buying followers:** Fake follower counts destroy engagement rate (ER = engagement/followers); a 100K account with fake followers shows 0.1% ER vs. genuine 2–4% — brands and the algorithm notice
- **Posting inconsistently:** The algorithm rewards consistent posting; 1 post/day with average quality beats sporadic posting of high-quality content
- **Using only Reels:** A mix of formats (Reel for reach, Carousel for saves, Stories for retention) is algorithmically healthier than Reels-only
- **Ignoring DMs and comments:** Response rate and speed in comments and DMs is an account health signal; unanswered comments signal low engagement to the algorithm
- **Copying aesthetics exactly:** Inspiration is fine; exact replication is a brand signal of no differentiation — audiences recognize authentic vs. derivative
- **Not tracking Instagram Shopping performance:** Attribution from Instagram Shopping tab vs. feed tags vs. Stories reveals which format drives purchase intent — optimize based on data

## Success Metrics

- **Reels reach rate:** Target average Reels reach equal to 10–30% of follower count (Reels should reach well beyond existing followers)
- **Stories completion rate:** 70%+ story sequence completion rate (frames watched through to last) indicates high follower quality and content relevance
- **Saves per post:** Carousel posts averaging 100+ saves per 10,000 followers indicates strong utility/reference value
- **Follower growth rate:** 3–8% month-over-month follower growth (organic, not from giveaways)
- **Instagram Shopping conversion:** 2–5% of product tag clicks converting to website purchase within 7-day attribution window
