## Platform Playbooks

### X / Twitter
- Replies (×13.5), retweets (×20), profile clicks (×12), bookmarks (×10) are weighted far higher than likes (×1)
- Post links in the first reply, not the main tweet — link posts get suppressed
- Threads get 3× more engagement than single tweets; sweet spot is 5-10 tweets (7 optimal)
- Video dominates: 4 out of 5 sessions include video consumption
- Optimal volume: 15-30 tweets/day; best times: weekdays 8-10 AM, 7-9 PM
- Engage in conversations on others' tweets to trigger algorithmic exposure

### LinkedIn
- Algorithm favors "knowledge and advice" — educational carousels and mini case studies win
- Document/PDF carousels: 2-3× more dwell time than text posts (1,387 avg impressions vs 589)
- First 60 minutes are critical — early engagement determines amplification
- 3-minute "micro lesson" posts get the strongest algorithmic push
- Best time: 7 AM weekdays
- Average engagement rate: ~3-3.5% (highest among major platforms)

### Instagram
- Reels are the primary growth vector — algorithm rewards original creative content
- Carousel posts and Reels outperform static images
- Video views grew 29% YoY — lean into short-form video
- Best times: 3-6 PM weekdays
- Average engagement rate: ~0.48%

### TikTok
- Algorithm is predictive — surfaces content before users search for it
- Sweet spot: 15-60 second videos
- Trending hashtags yield 23% higher engagement
- Average engagement rate: 3.70% (highest platform; up 49% YoY)
- Pattern interruption in first 2 seconds dramatically improves completion rates
- Strict penalties for bot-like activity — can cost 50% organic visibility

## Content Creation

**Hook Formulas (first 3 seconds matter):**
- Bold claim: "Most people get this wrong about..."
- Provocative question: "What if I told you..."
- Pattern interrupt: visual or auditory break in the first 2 seconds
- Contrarian take: state popular opinion, then challenge it

**Storytelling Frameworks:**
- **PAS** (Problem-Agitate-Solution) — tweet threads, carousel posts
- **BAB** (Before-After-Bridge) — case studies, testimonials
- **Contrarian** — state popular opinion, challenge, provide evidence. Best for thought leadership
- **Hook-Build Tension-Reveal-Payoff** — educational content, behind-the-scenes

**Thread Structure:**
1. Bold claim or question (the hook)
2. Promise/stakes
3. Thread marker ("A thread 🧵")
4. Body with visual breaks every 3-4 tweets (charts, screenshots increase completion by 45%)
5. Clear CTA (follow, retweet, bookmark)

## Engagement & Community

- **Response speed matters:** engage during the first hour after posting ("golden hour")
- **Respond to every comment** within 3 hours — 76% of consumers feel more loyal to brands that reply
- **Proactive over reactive:** initiate conversations, don't just respond
- **Comment-first strategy:** thoughtfully reply to other creators' posts to grow visibility
- **Authenticity signal:** raw, phone-shot content outperforms studio production
- **Community over virality:** deep resonance in smaller engaged communities beats chasing one-off viral hits

## Content Calendar

**Frequency guidelines:**
- Quality beats quantity: 3 high-quality posts/week > 7 mediocre ones
- X/Twitter is the exception: high volume (15-30/day) rewards reach
- Leave 20-30% of calendar slots flexible for real-time trend riding

**Calendar entries should include:** date/time, platform, content type, copy, hashtags, media assets, links, mentions

**Content mix:** educational, entertaining, promotional, community-driven, trend-responsive

## Hashtag Strategy

**Formula:** 1-2 broad tags + 2-3 niche tags + 1-2 branded/campaign tags

**Platform-specific limits:**
- TikTok: hashtags strongly influence For You Page — use generously
- Instagram: 5-10 targeted hashtags (quality over quantity)
- LinkedIn: 3-5 relevant professional tags
- X/Twitter: 1-2 max — more reduces engagement

**Trend riding:** Monitor daily. Jump on trends early in their lifecycle. Adapt to your niche rather than copying generically.

## Copywriting

**Character limits:**
- X: 280 (non-Premium) / 25,000 (Premium)
- Instagram: 2,200 caption
- TikTok: 4,000 caption (use for SEO — helps algorithm categorize)
- LinkedIn: 3,000
- YouTube: 5,000 description

**Writing rules:**
- One big idea per post — don't dilute
- Front-load value in the first line
- Use line breaks aggressively — no walls of text
- 6th-8th grade reading level
- Contractions, short sentences, definitive stance

**CTAs:** Short and action-driven. "Save this," "Share with a friend," "Drop a 🔥 if you agree." Vary by platform — links work on LinkedIn/X, "link in bio" for Instagram.

**Emoji:** 1-3 per post as visual anchors. More acceptable on Instagram/TikTok, less on LinkedIn/X.

## Growth Strategies

- **Native content always wins** — every platform penalizes outbound links
- **Early engagement velocity** (first 30-60 min) determines distribution
- **Consistency** signals reliability to algorithms — sporadic posting gets deprioritized
- **Cross-post adapted content** (not identical copies — tailor format and tone)
- **Never use engagement pods/bots** — platforms enforce strict penalties

## Brand Voice

- Define 3-5 core traits with Do/Don't examples
- Maintain a swipe file of on-brand post examples
- Audit monthly for voice drift
